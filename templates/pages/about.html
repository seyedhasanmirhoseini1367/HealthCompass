About HealthCompass

Data · Insight · Care

HealthCompass brings your medical records, reminders, appointments and the people who help you into one place, and lets you ask questions about your own health in plain language.

What it does

  • Records. Upload lab results and documents; they are read, organised and searchable.
  • AI Assistant. Ask about your own records or general health topics. Answers cite where they came from.
  • Reminders and appointments. Medication reminders you confirm, and visits with reminders before them.
  • Companions. Share chosen parts of your health information with family or carers, and look after others.
  • For clinicians and staff. Patient overviews for doctors, an experimental AI scribe, and analytics tools for the team.

Status

HealthCompass is a prototype under active development. Features change, and some are marked experimental. It is not a medical device; please read the medical disclaimer.
