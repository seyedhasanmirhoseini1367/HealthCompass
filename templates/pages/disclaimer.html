Medical disclaimer

Please read before relying on anything you see here

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Finland and the EU). Do not wait for an app.

Not medical advice

HealthCompass helps you organise and understand health information. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a medical device. Nothing here replaces a qualified health professional who knows you.

AI can be wrong

  • Answers, summaries, alerts and predictions are generated automatically. They can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, even when they cite a source.
  • Guideline excerpts are shown for context and may not apply to your situation.
  • Scribe notes are drafts. A clinician must check them before they are used.

Reminders and alerts

Medication reminders and health alerts are aids, not a safety system. A missing alert does not mean everything is fine. Do not change or stop treatment based on this app; ask your doctor or pharmacist.

Your records

Information is only as accurate as what was uploaded and how it was read. Check important values against the original document.
