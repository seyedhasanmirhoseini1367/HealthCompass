Privacy

How HealthCompass handles your information

Short version: we keep what you put in, use it to run the features you choose, send health data to an outside AI provider only if you allow it, and you can download or delete everything at any time.

What we store

InformationWhy
Account details: name, email, role, password (hashed) or Google sign-inTo let you sign in and show the right pages for your role
Health information you add: records, documents, measurements, reminders, appointments, messages to the assistantTo provide the features you use
Sharing settings: who can see what, and for how longSo companions and clinicians see only what you allowed
Consent decisions, with date and versionTo respect and prove what you agreed to
Security and audit logs: sign-ins, access to recordsTo protect accounts and investigate misuse

Sensitive identifiers are encrypted in the database. Uploaded files are only served back to people allowed to see them.

AI processing and outside providers

Some features use AI. Each use is a separate choice in your consent settings:

  • AI analysis of my health data and automated reading of my documents: processing to organise and summarise your records.
  • Sending my health data to external AI providers: the assistant and scribe send the relevant text to a language-model provider (Groq, Google, Anthropic or OpenAI) to write an answer. Without this consent, those features do not send your health data out.
  • Sharing with linked clinicians and anonymised research are separate choices too.

You can withdraw any consent at any time. Withdrawing stops future processing for that purpose.

Who else handles data

The service runs on a cloud hosting provider and a managed database provider. Google is used if you choose Google sign-in. The AI providers above receive data only as described. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

HealthCompass sets only the cookies it needs to work: one to keep you signed in, one to protect forms against forgery, and short-lived ones for on-screen messages. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Your choices

  • Download your data at any time.
  • See and revoke who can see your information.
  • Delete your account and the data in it.
  • Change your consent settings.
