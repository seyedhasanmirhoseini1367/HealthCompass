Terms of use

The rules for using HealthCompass

The service

HealthCompass is a prototype for organising personal health information and exploring it with AI. It is provided as is, may change or be unavailable at times, and is not a medical service. See the medical disclaimer.

Your account

  • Give accurate details and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Doctor, hospital and staff accounts are approved before they get access to other people's information.
  • Tell us if you think someone else has used your account.

What you upload

  • Only upload information you are entitled to share. For someone else's data, you need their permission.
  • You keep ownership of what you upload. You let HealthCompass store and process it to run the features you use.
  • Keep your own copies of important documents. Download your data at any time from Settings.

Acceptable use

Do not try to access other people's data without permission, disrupt the service, probe it for weaknesses without agreement, or use it for anything unlawful.

Clinicians and staff

Professional users see patient information only as patients have allowed, and remain responsible for their own clinical judgement and professional obligations. AI output, including scribe notes, is a draft to be checked, never a final record.

Ending use

You can delete your account at any time. Accounts that misuse the service may be suspended.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, HealthCompass is not liable for decisions made on the basis of information in the service, or for loss caused by interruptions or errors.
